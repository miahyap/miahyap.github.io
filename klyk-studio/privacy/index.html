Klyk Studio

Privacy

Last updated 2 November 2026

Klyk Studio doesn't collect any data. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it never contacts a server of its own.

Your recordings, presets, setlists and settings stay on your devices. If you use iCloud, they sync through your own private iCloud account, which the developer can't access.

The microphone is used only to tune, to record and to find the tempo of your playing, and only while you're using those features. Recordings never leave your devices or your iCloud unless you export or share them.

Bluetooth and the local network are used only to connect the MIDI controllers, footswitches and network MIDI sessions you choose.

Subscriptions are handled by the App Store. Klyk Studio learns only whether a subscription is active, never who you are or how you pay.

To delete your data, delete the app. If you use iCloud, also remove Klyk Studio's data in your iCloud storage settings.

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/miahyap/miahyap.github.io.
